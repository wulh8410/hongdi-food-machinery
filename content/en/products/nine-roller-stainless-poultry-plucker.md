The 9-roller stainless poultry plucker is built for higher-capacity poultry dehairing. Its transparent PVC panel helps operators observe machine operation, while caster wheels improve mobility.

Compared with small poultry pluckers, the 9-roller model supports larger processing volume and more convenient observation, movement, and maintenance.
