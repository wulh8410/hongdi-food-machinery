## Answer
Safe operation depends on the exact machine, its guarding and controls, correct grounding, a water-resistant electrical installation, operator training, and power isolation before cleaning or maintenance.

## Buying Notes
Before ordering, confirm the guard arrangement, control position, emergency-stop or disconnect method, supply voltage, circuit protection, grounding, and cleaning procedure for the selected model. Do not reach into moving parts.

## Information to Provide
- Product type and average weight
- Daily output and peak-hour output
- Site size, water source, drainage, and voltage
- Whether scalding, dehairing, washing, or trimming equipment is already available
- Budget range and expected delivery arrangement
