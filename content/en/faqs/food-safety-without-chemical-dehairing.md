## Answer
Non-food chemicals must not be used in food production. If feathers remain, correct the process first: scalding temperature and time, rubber-finger condition, loading quantity, water replacement, drainage, and cleaning.

## Buying Notes
Confirm that food-contact surfaces are cleanable, waste feathers are intercepted before drainage, and cleaning and disinfection follow the operator's applicable food-production requirements. The cited national rules are a baseline; local licensing and inspection requirements still apply.

## Information to Provide
- Product type and average weight
- Daily output and peak-hour output
- Site size, water source, drainage, and voltage
- Whether scalding, dehairing, washing, or trimming equipment is already available
- Budget range and expected delivery arrangement
