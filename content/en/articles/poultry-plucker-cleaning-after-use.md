## Direct Answer
After daily use, power off the machine first, then remove feathers, skin residue, and wastewater. Check rubber fingers, drum walls, drainage areas, and transmission parts.

## Recommended Cleaning Order
Stop the machine and disconnect power before cleaning. Remove remaining feathers first, then rinse the drum wall, rubber fingers, and bottom drainage area. If oil, skin residue, or fine feathers build up, add brushing to the cleaning process.

## Key Areas to Check
Dirt often remains between rubber fingers, near the drum bottom, around the outlet, and under the machine. Poor cleaning can affect dehairing results, create odor, and speed up wear.

## What to Avoid
Do not spray electrical parts, switches, or motors with high-pressure water for long periods. Keep electrical areas dry and leave the machine ventilated after cleaning.

## Before Buying, Check
- Whether the machine is easy to rinse
- Whether the drainage outlet fits the site
- Whether rubber fingers are easy to replace
- Whether the drum has difficult cleaning corners
- Whether spare parts are available
