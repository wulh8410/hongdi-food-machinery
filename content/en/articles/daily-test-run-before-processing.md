## Direct Answer
Yes. A short daily test is recommended. Check sound, rotation, switches, and then confirm scalding and plucking results with a small amount of material.

## Why Test First
After cleaning, storage, or moving, rubber fingers may loosen, residue may remain, drainage may be blocked, or power contact may be unstable. A few minutes of checking can prevent downtime during peak hours.

## No-load Check
Check whether startup is smooth, rotation is stable, sound is normal, and switches work correctly. Do not load birds if obvious noise, jamming, or overheating appears.

## Material Test
Bird species, weight, and scalding condition may change each day. A small material test helps adjust water temperature, scalding time, batch size, and plucking time.

## Simple Checklist
- No foreign objects in the drum
- Rubber fingers are not missing or cracked
- Drainage is clear
- Power cable and switch are normal
- No abnormal no-load sound
