## Direct Answer
Drainage blockage is usually caused by site layout, drainage slope, feather collection, and cleaning rhythm working poorly together.

## Common Causes
Poultry dehairing produces feathers, skin residue, and wastewater. If feathers flow directly into the floor drain, blockage can happen quickly. Ducks and geese create more feather waste, and small drains or poor floor slope make the issue worse.

## Practical Site Suggestions
Add a simple collection basket, filter screen, or settling bucket near the outlet. Let feathers be collected before wastewater enters the drain. Floor drainage should guide water away from the equipment area.

## Layout Matters
Scalding, plucking, trimming, and washing should be arranged in processing order. A clear workflow reduces handling effort and keeps wastewater in an easier-to-clean area.

## Information to Prepare
- Drainage direction
- Floor drain or drainage channel position
- Daily poultry volume
- Main species handled
- Whether mobile equipment is needed
